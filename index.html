Each year, Riada American School's Model United Nations program brings student delegates into formal committee — debating the issues shaping our world in the language of resolutions, points of order, and the floor. This year, for the first time, RASMUN opens its doors to delegations from other schools. Below are the current conference and the archive of the one before it — more will join them each year.
