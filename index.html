SIGNATURE ACADEMIC PROGRAM

Riada American School Model United Nations (RAS MUN)

RAS MUN provides students with an authentic simulation of the United Nations, where they develop the knowledge, skills, and dispositions needed to become informed global citizens and future leaders. Through research, debate, negotiation, and collaboration, students learn to understand complex international issues while practicing diplomacy and respectful dialogue.

RAS MUN is designed to extend learning beyond the classroom by encouraging students to think critically about global challenges and to appreciate the importance of international cooperation in finding peaceful and sustainable solutions.

Developing Global Citizens

Students examine issues such as climate change, poverty, human rights, international security, public health, sustainable development, and migration through multiple cultural and political perspectives.

By representing different countries, students learn to:

✓ Understand diverse viewpoints and cultures ✓ Respect differences in political, economic, and social systems
✓ Develop empathy for people facing global challenges ✓ Appreciate the value of international cooperation
✓ Recognize their role as responsible global citizens  

Strengthening Critical Thinking and Research Skills

MUN requires students to investigate real-world issues using credible sources, analyze complex information, and develop evidence-based positions.

Participants develop the ability to:

✓ Conduct independent research ✓ Evaluate reliable sources of information
✓ Analyze international issues objectively ✓ Develop policy positions supported by evidence
✓ Formulate practical and balanced solutions  

These research skills support success across all academic disciplines.

Developing Communication and Public Speaking

Delegates prepare speeches, present policy statements, engage in debate, negotiate resolutions, and respond to questions from fellow delegates.

Students develop confidence in:

✓ Public speaking ✓ Persuasive communication
✓ Active listening ✓ Formal debate
✓ Diplomatic negotiation ✓ Professional presentation skills
✓ Writing position papers and resolutions  

These communication skills prepare students for university studies, professional careers, and leadership roles.

Promoting Diplomacy and Collaboration

Unlike traditional debates that focus on winning an argument, MUN emphasizes diplomacy, consensus-building, and cooperation.

Participants practice:

✓ Negotiation and compromise ✓ Consensus-building
✓ Conflict resolution ✓ Collaborative problem-solving
✓ Respectful disagreement ✓ Leadership through service

These experiences teach students that effective leaders seek solutions that benefit the broader community.

Building Leadership and Confidence

Whether serving as delegates, committee chairs, or conference organizers, students learn to manage responsibilities, make informed decisions, and work effectively under pressure.

The program helps students develop:

✓ Leadership skills ✓ Self-confidence
✓ Independence ✓ Organization and time management
✓ Decision-making abilities ✓ Adaptability and resilience

As students become more experienced, they often mentor younger participants, fostering a culture of leadership throughout the school.

Encouraging Ethical Leadership

MUN reinforces the importance of integrity, responsibility, respect, and ethical decision-making — considering both national interests and the broader needs of the international community.

Through discussions of global issues, students gain a deeper appreciation for:

✓ Human rights ✓ Social justice
✓ Peacebuilding ✓ Environmental stewardship
✓ Sustainable development ✓ Humanitarian responsibility

These values align closely with the character education and Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) goals of Riada American School.

Academic and Career Preparation

Participation in MUN prepares students for higher education and future careers by developing many of the competencies sought by universities and employers. Students strengthen skills in:

✓ Research and analysis ✓ Writing and documentation
✓ Public speaking ✓ Leadership
✓ Collaboration ✓ Critical thinking
✓ Problem-solving ✓ Professional communication

These transferable skills support future studies in fields such as international relations, political science, law, business, economics, journalism, public policy, diplomacy, environmental science, and international development.

Expected Outcomes

Through participation in the RAS Model United Nations Program, students will:

✓ Develop a deeper understanding of global affairs and international organizations ✓ Strengthen research, writing, and analytical skills
✓ Communicate confidently through formal speeches and debate ✓ Demonstrate respectful dialogue and diplomatic negotiation
✓ Work collaboratively to solve complex global problems ✓ Build leadership, confidence, and professional communication skills
✓ Appreciate diverse cultures and perspectives ✓ Demonstrate ethical decision-making and global responsibility
✓ Become informed, compassionate, and engaged global citizens  

Preparing Students for a Global Future

At Riada American School, the Model United Nations program reflects our commitment to preparing students for success in an increasingly interconnected world. By engaging with real-world issues through the lens of diplomacy and collaboration, students learn that meaningful change begins with informed dialogue, mutual respect, and a willingness to work together.

RAS MUN empowers students to become thoughtful leaders who can analyze complex challenges, communicate effectively across cultures, and contribute positively to their communities and the global society — prepared to lead with integrity and purpose.

About Riada MUN

Admin / 16 September 2024 / Branding

Model UN is a form of simulation-based education. Students are assigned different roles to interpret and role-play with other student participants. These roles can vary from pretending to represent a single country to a political figure. Model UN activates students’ imagination and creativity - activities that students are naturally inclined to do.

The Model United Nations at Riada American School allows students to participate in conferences around Egypt and to express their thoughts and debates on global issues within the context of their country delegations.
